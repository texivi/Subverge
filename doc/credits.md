# Credits

[texivi](https://github.com/texivi) wrote Subverge.

## Inspiration

The idea came from [TempleOS](https://templeos.org), by Terry A. Davis. Its shell is also its compiler, its programs all run in ring 0, and it is small enough for one person to understand. Subverge uses none of its code. The site at templeos.org has the system and what Terry A. Davis wrote about it.

The infinite values in omega came from Vsauce's video [How To Count Past Infinity](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SrU9YDoXE88). Ordinals and cardinals are values in the language because of it, and the kernel and the shell are named after them.

## Other software

GRUB boots the ISO and is the only outside code that Subverge runs. It is built with gcc, binutils and nasm, and the ISO is made with `grub-mkrescue` and `xorriso`.

## Fonts

The text is drawn with the 8 by 16 VGA font that the video card provides, with a few characters added by the kernel for `ω`, `ℵ` and subscript digits.

## The haelide theme

The `haelide` theme and its logo are inspired by HaliadeSYS, made by gretagen: https://github.com/gretagen/HaliadeSYS. See [themes](themes) and [logos](logos).

## Music

The songs of [sound](sound) are arrangements of compositions that are in the public domain: Ode to Joy and Fur Elise by Ludwig van Beethoven, the Prelude in C major of the first book of The Well-Tempered Clavier by Johann Sebastian Bach, the first Gymnopedie by Erik Satie, the Canon in D by Johann Pachelbel and the traditional Greensleeves. The notes were written out for Subverge.
